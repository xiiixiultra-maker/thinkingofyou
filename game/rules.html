The Long Way Home

Official Rules

A high-score contest for the adidas ADIZERO Evo SL “Thinking of You” release at Shoe Gallery. No purchase necessary. Open to US residents 18 and older.

Who runs it

Shoe Gallery, 1060 NE 79th St, Miami, Florida (“we”). It’s a game of skill: the best runs make the final, and the final decides the winner. There is no drawing.

When

  • The contest opens Thursday, November 12, 2026 at 9:00 AM ET and the board closes Saturday, November 14, 2026 at 3:00 PM ET.
  • Runs before the contest opens are practice. The board resets to zero when it opens.
  • The live final is 4:00 PM ET and the winner is announced at 5:00 PM ET on Saturday, November 14.

Who can play to win

  • Legal residents of the 50 United States and Washington, DC who are 18 or older when they play.
  • Employees of Shoe Gallery and adidas, and their immediate families and households, can’t win.
  • Void where prohibited.

How to enter

  • Play The Long Way Home at thinkingofyou.shoegallery.app/game on your phone, or on the store tablet at Shoe Gallery.
  • Put a name on the board and give us your Instagram handle or phone number. Your name shows on the board. Your Instagram or phone never does.
  • It’s free. Buying anything won’t help you win.
  • Play as often as you like. Only your best run counts. One person, one name, one Instagram or phone.

Playing fair

  • No bots, scripts, changed game files or anything else that plays or scores for you.
  • Very high scores can be held for a quick check before they go on the board.
  • We can take any run off the board, or disqualify a player, if we believe it wasn’t a fair run. Our decisions are final.

The finalists

  • When the board closes at 3:00 PM ET, the three best scores on the board are the finalists.
  • We message each one through the Instagram handle or phone number they gave us. A finalist has to reply by 3:30 PM ET, or the next best score takes their place.

The final

  • At 4:00 PM ET each finalist plays one run: in the store on their own phone, shown on the store TV, or on a live video call with their screen in view.
  • The best score in the final wins. In a tie, fewer misses wins, then the better qualifying score.
  • If the game crashes or the call drops, the finalist gets one more run. If it happens again, their qualifying score counts as their final run.

Prizes

  • 1st: one pair of adidas ADIZERO Evo SL “Thinking of You” in the winner’s size, while that size is available (retail value $150).
  • 2nd and 3rd: Shoe Gallery merch, a tee and a pair of socks.
  • Winners in Miami can pick up at the store. We ship free anywhere in the US.
  • Prizes have no cash value and can’t be traded or transferred. If a prize isn’t available, we may swap in one of equal or greater value. Winners pay any taxes on their prize.

Your information

  • We only use your Instagram or phone to reach you if you make the final. We delete them after the contest.
  • Unless the law says otherwise, the winner agrees we can share their board name and a photo from the final on Shoe Gallery’s social media.

The fine print

  • We may pause, change or end the contest if it can’t run as planned, for example if the game is broken or hacked. If that happens, we may pick the finalists from the fair scores before the problem.
  • We aren’t responsible for lost connections, phone or network trouble, or lag.
  • This contest isn’t sponsored, endorsed or run by Instagram or Meta.

Questions

Ask us in the store, or send Shoe Gallery a DM on Instagram.
